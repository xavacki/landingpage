No need to reinstall the game.You can request deletion by email from the account address, even if the app is no longer on your device.

1. Request deletion by email

Email xavacki@gmail.com from the Google email address used to sign in to Gravity Throttle. Use the subject “Gravity Throttle account deletion” and tell us you want the game account and cloud data deleted. We may verify ownership. Never send a password or payment-card number.

Request account deletion ↗

This opens your email application. If it does not open, copy the address above into your email service.

2. Delete from the game

Open Settings → Google account → Delete account and confirm. This requires an internet connection. For security, you may be asked to sign out and sign in again before confirming. The control is available in game builds containing account deletion; older builds can use the email route.

The server blocks new account wallet operations once deletion starts. Interrupted server cleanup is retried automatically. If the app cannot confirm deletion, retry while connected or contact us. For email requests we respond without undue delay, normally within one month under applicable GDPR rules, and explain any lawful extension within that period.

3. What is deleted

  • Your Firebase game account and cloud coins, upgrades, race records and paid level entitlement.
  • Account-linked wallet operation records, including nested receipts.
  • The account UID and order ID links in our game purchase-verification ledger.
  • On the requesting device, account caches and queued operations after confirmation. Other devices clear account caches when they reconnect and the server rejects the old session.

Deletion cannot be undone. Paid coins and level access are not restored to a new account. Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account, erase Google’s independent transaction records, or automatically refund purchases.

4. What remains and why

Minimal purchase-token hashes, product amounts/status, revocation/deletion markers and timestamps are retained for the lifetime of our verification service to prevent purchase reuse. A hashed account identifier and deletion-completion timestamps block stale requests. These are pseudonymous fraud-prevention records; they are not described as anonymous.

Separate guest saves stay on your devices. Offline devices may retain cached information until reconnection or app-storage removal. Clear guest/local data through Android storage controls or uninstalling, subject to platform backup behaviour.

Analytics choice and crash diagnostics are separate. Account deletion does not automatically erase all installation-based Analytics/Crashlytics reports or change your device’s Analytics choice. Use Settings → Analytics & privacy to stop optional Analytics and contact us for data-rights requests. Retention and provider deletion processing are explained in the privacy policy.
