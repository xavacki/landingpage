Delete your Gravity Throttle account

You can request deletion here even if you no longer have the game installed.

Request deletion by email

Email xavacki@gmail.com from the Google email address you use to sign in to Gravity Throttle. Use the subject “Gravity Throttle account deletion” and state that you want your game account and cloud data deleted. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Never send your password or payment card details.

Request account deletion

Delete from the game

Open Settings → Google account → Delete account and confirm. For security, you may need to sign out and sign in again before confirming. An internet connection is required.

What is deleted

Your Firebase game account, cloud coins, upgrades, race records, level entitlement, and account-linked wallet operation records are deleted. Account links and order IDs are removed from the game's purchase-verification ledger. The requesting device clears its account cache after confirmation. Other devices clear their cache when they reconnect and the server rejects their old session.

What is retained

Unlinked purchase-token hashes and minimal purchase/revocation records are retained for as long as the purchase-verification service operates to prevent previously consumed or refunded purchases being reused. A hashed account identifier and deletion-completion timestamps are retained to block stale requests. This is pseudonymous fraud-prevention data, not a claim of anonymous data.

Separate guest saves stay on your devices. Remove these through Android app-storage controls or uninstalling. Analytics and crash-report retention are described in the privacy policy. Deleting your game account does not delete your Google account, erase Google's transaction records, or automatically refund a purchase. Purchased coins and level access are lost and are not restored to a new game account.
