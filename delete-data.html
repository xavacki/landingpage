Keep your account. No need to reinstall. Use the email request below to ask for data deletion without deleting your Gravity Throttle account. For account deletion instead, use the separate account-deletion page.

1. Request data deletion

Email xavacki@gmail.com with the subject “Gravity Throttle data deletion — keep my account”. If you use cloud saves, send the request from the Google email address used to sign in to the game.

  1. Tell us which data you want deleted, such as saved race records and gameplay progress, or a full gameplay and wallet reset.
  2. Say explicitly that you want to keep your game account.
  3. We may verify account ownership and will explain the scope, any retained records, and the effect on coins or upgrades before carrying out the request.
Request data deletion ↗

This opens your email application; it does not send a request automatically. If it does not open, copy the email address and subject above. Never send a password, Google sign-in code, or payment-card details.

2. Reset from the game

For a full gameplay reset, open Settings → Reset all progress and read the confirmation before continuing. If you want to reset cloud progress, first sign in to the relevant Google account and stay connected until the game confirms the reset.

A full reset also resets your coin balance and upgrades. This includes unspent purchased coins. It keeps your game account and verified paid level access, and does not refund purchases or make previously consumed coin packs available again. If you only want specific data deleted, use the email request instead.

Do not choose Delete account if you want to keep the account. Signing out, uninstalling, or clearing local app storage does not delete cloud data.

4. Timing and retention

Email requests are reviewed manually, not processed instantly. We normally respond within one month. If verification, provider processing, or an applicable lawful extension is needed, we explain the next steps and timing. We confirm when the agreed request has been completed.

In-game cloud resets need an internet connection and server confirmation. Other devices may retain cached data until they reconnect and sync. Data removed by a reset is not promised to disappear immediately from every offline device or provider backup.

  • Account information: kept while you maintain the account, unless you separately request account deletion. There is no automatic inactivity-deletion schedule.
  • Wallet operation receipts: account-linked operation types and timestamps remain with the account until account deletion.
  • Purchase and fraud-prevention records: retained for the lifetime of the verification service to validate purchases, handle refunds and prevent reuse. Keeping your account also keeps its purchase links.
  • Analytics, crash reports and operational logs: follow their separate retention periods; a gameplay reset does not erase them. See the privacy policy’s retention table, including the configured Analytics retention window, Crashlytics processing, and cloud log periods.
  • Request correspondence: retained while handling your request, then only as needed for follow-up, disputes, evidence of fulfilment or a legal obligation, as described in the privacy policy.

We do not promise that all user data is automatically deleted within 90 days.

5. Other data and guest saves

For other personal-data deletion requests, email xavacki@gmail.com and describe the data you mean. We will explain what can be identified and deleted, any verification needed, and any lawful retention. Analytics and crash diagnostics can be associated with an installation rather than your Google-linked game account, so account identity alone may not identify every report.

You can stop optional Analytics through Settings → Analytics & privacy. Changing consent stops future optional collection; it is not a promise that all previously collected reports are immediately erased.

If you play as a guest, saves are stored on your device. Use Settings → Reset all progress for a gameplay reset, or Android’s app-storage controls for local app data. We cannot remotely erase files that exist only on your device. Android backups may have their own behaviour. A separate guest save is not automatically removed by resetting a signed-in cloud account.
