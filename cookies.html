Website data & cookies

A straightforward notice about browsing the redesigned xavacki website. Effective 29 October 2026.

Updated 29 October 2026

1. Cookies and browser storage

The redesigned studio and legal pages do not set cookies or use local storage, session storage, website analytics, advertising pixels or fingerprinting scripts. There is no website advertising personalisation or marketing consent choice to collect through these pages.

The game is separate: it has local saves, authentication and optional Analytics as described in the privacy policy. Turning a browser setting on or off does not control the game’s SDKs.

2. Hosting and external resources

The website’s images, styles and font are self-hosted with the page. The redesign removes the previous external Google Fonts and JavaScript CDN dependencies. No embedded video, map or social widget loads automatically.

GitHub Pages serves the website and processes technical request/security information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Host/CDN infrastructure can process IP addresses and access information even though these pages do not run trackers. External links and your email provider have their own practices when you choose to use them.

3. Changes and contact

If we introduce optional website tracking in the future, this notice and any required consent controls must be updated before that tracking starts. Questions: xavacki@gmail.com. See the operator details.
