2. Information used by Gravity Throttle

The current Gravity Throttle game works without a player account. The game code does not automatically send gameplay records or sensor readings to Xavacki Games, and does not currently include an advertising, analytics, or remote crash-reporting SDK.

  • Local game files: settings, selected content, progress, unlocks, and best times are saved in the app’s local storage so you can continue playing.
  • Tilt steering: when you enable it, the game reads accelerometer measurements to control the bike. These readings are processed on your device and are not saved as a sensor history or sent to us by the game.
  • Vibration: the vibration setting controls phone feedback when a gameplay button is pressed. It does not provide us with personal information.
  • Selected files: where file import is available, the app reads the level file you choose through the system picker and stores an app-local copy. It does not use that selection to upload the file to us.

The operating system, app store, or a document provider you choose may separately process information. Android may also back up app files depending on your device and account settings. These platform features are distinct from a cloud-save service operated by Xavacki Games.
