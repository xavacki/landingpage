Who is responsible

Gravity Throttle (Android package com.xavacki.gravitythrottle) is provided by Oleh Makhobei, an individual based in Slovakia, publishing under the developer name xavacki. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to that developer.

For privacy questions or requests, contact xavacki@gmail.com. Postal contact: Bozeny Nemcovej 12, 94501 Komarno, Slovakia (SK).

This policy covers the Android game and its account, cloud-save and purchase services. A website hosting this policy may process technical access information under its own applicable privacy notice.

Information the game handles

Local gameplay

The game stores settings, selected bikes, upgrades, coin balances, level progress and best race times on your device. Guest gameplay does not require a Google account. If you enable tilt steering, accelerometer readings control the bike locally; the game does not upload a history of those readings. Imported custom level files remain local to the game.

Android may back up app data according to your device and Google account settings. Local gameplay is separate from the analytics and diagnostics described below, which can operate even when you are signed out.

Google sign-in and cloud saves

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase Authentication process your Google account identifier, email address and profile information made available through sign-in, such as your name and profile picture. Firebase assigns an account identifier and processes authentication information, including IP addresses and technical request information, for sign-in and abuse prevention. We do not receive your Google password.

We use your game account identifier to store and synchronize coins, bike unlocks, upgrades, level-access purchases, completed-level reward claims, race records and related timestamps. This lets you continue using the same account on another device and helps prevent duplicate rewards and purchase delivery. Your email address is displayed in the game's account dialog.

In-app purchases

Google Play processes checkout and payment details. We do not receive your full payment-card number or bank credentials. The game sends the selected product identifier and Google Play purchase token to our verification service. The service checks the purchase with Google Play before delivering coins or level access.

We keep a record containing your game account identifier, product identifier, Google Play order identifier where available, purchase-token hash, delivery information and timestamps. An account-derived identifier is also supplied to Google Play to associate purchases with the correct game account. Pending purchase tokens can be kept in app storage until delivery is confirmed. These records help restore eligible entitlements, resolve purchase problems and prevent repeated credit for the same purchase.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used and improve it. Information includes app and installation identifiers, app version, device and operating-system information, sessions and gameplay interactions. Game events include screens visited, levels started and finished, race duration, crashes within gameplay, bike class, coin rewards and spending, and shop interactions.

Google Analytics may also process identifiers, approximate location derived from network information and other automatically collected information according to its configuration and device settings. We do not request precise GPS location. Our custom gameplay events do not include your email address, Google account name or Firebase account identifier.

In the current version, analytics collection is enabled automatically when the app starts. The game does not currently present an analytics consent prompt or provide an in-app setting to disable analytics. Signing out of your Google account does not disable analytics collection. For questions about analytics data or to exercise applicable privacy rights, contact xavacki@gmail.com.

Crash reports and technical diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics, including native crash reporting, to diagnose software failures. Reports can include stack traces, technical device and app information, installation identifiers, session information, game/renderer configuration and events preceding a failure. These reports help identify and fix stability problems.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond and investigate your request. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Why we use information

We use information to operate the game, authenticate accounts, synchronize progress, verify and deliver purchases, prevent abuse, provide support, understand gameplay and fix technical problems. We also process information when needed to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where European data-protection law applies, our legal bases include performance of the services you request for account, cloud-save and purchase features; legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and responding to support requests; and applicable legal obligations. Where consent is required for analytics or other optional processing, that processing must use the consent arrangements described in the released app. Using the game or reading this policy does not itself provide consent.

Service providers and disclosure

We use Google services, including Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These providers process information to deliver the functions described above. Google Play and your Google account are also governed by Google's own terms and privacy notices.

We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent fraud or exercise legal rights. The current game does not integrate an advertising network. We do not sell players' personal information.

Where information is processed and how it is protected

Our game wallet database and verification functions are configured in Google's Belgium region (europe-west1). Other Google services may process information outside Slovakia and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. A regional database does not mean all processing remains in the EU. Where required, international transfers are subject to the relevant provider's contractual safeguards and applicable data-protection law.

App requests to our cloud services use encrypted HTTPS connections. Server operations require an authenticated account; purchases are verified on the server, and the database restricts direct client access. Access is limited to authorized administrators and service providers. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention

Local saves remain on your device until removed through the game's controls, Android app-storage controls or uninstalling, subject to platform backup behavior. Clearing local data or signing out does not automatically delete your cloud account.

Cloud account, progress and wallet data are retained while your account is maintained, until an account-deletion request is fulfilled. The current service has no automatic inactivity-deletion schedule.
