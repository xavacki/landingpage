3. Information handled by the game

Local play

The game stores settings, coins, upgrades, bike selection, race progress and best times on your device. Tilt steering uses accelerometer readings locally; we do not upload a history of those readings. Imported custom level files stay local. Android may back up app data according to your device and Google account settings.

Google sign-in and cloud saves

Google sign-in is optional. Google and Firebase Authentication process your Google identifier, email and profile information provided through sign-in, authentication tokens, IP addresses and technical security information. Firebase assigns a game account identifier. We never receive your Google password.

Our cloud service associates that identifier with coins, upgrades, reward claims, paid level access, race records, revisions, operation receipts and timestamps. It synchronizes progress across devices. The account dialog displays your email. Guest saves are separate from cloud saves; signing out does not delete your cloud account.

Purchases, refunds and fraud prevention

Google Play handles checkout and payment credentials. We do not receive your full card number or bank password. Our verifier receives the product ID and Play purchase token and checks the purchase with Google. The ledger stores a token hash, account UID, product, coin delivery amount, order ID when available, test status and timestamps. A hashed account identifier is provided to Play to bind a purchase to the correct game account. Pending tokens can remain in the device’s delivery queue.

Google Play notifications and purchase checks report cancellations, refunds and chargebacks. We update purchase status, coin balances, level entitlement and any coin shortfall from already-spent refunded packs. These are automated game-account adjustments; contact us if you believe one is wrong. See our purchase terms.

4. Optional analytics and crash diagnostics

Optional gameplay analytics

In builds with the Analytics & privacy control, Firebase Analytics collection is off until you choose Allow analytics. You can choose No thanks and still play, use cloud saves and make eligible purchases. Change or withdraw your choice at Settings → Analytics & privacy → No thanks. The choice is stored on your device; a new device may ask again.

If enabled, Analytics processes app and installation identifiers, app/device and operating-system information, sessions, screens viewed, level attempts and outcomes, race duration, bike class, coin rewards/spending and shop interactions. Firebase may derive approximate location and other technical information under its SDK configuration. Our custom events do not include your name, email or Firebase account UID. The app does not request precise GPS location. Advertising ID collection and advertising storage/personalization consent are disabled in these builds.

Earlier builds: versions without this setting start Analytics automatically and have no in-app analytics opt-out. The website cannot change an installed version. Update to a build containing the control; contact us with privacy requests concerning earlier collection. Reading this policy or accepting the terms does not provide analytics consent.

Withdrawing consent stops future Analytics collection; it does not automatically erase all historical reports. Contact us to request deletion of data that can be identified, providing only the information reasonably needed to locate it. We do not routinely link gameplay Analytics events to your signed-in account.

Crash diagnostics are separate

Firebase Crashlytics is enabled separately from optional gameplay Analytics. It reports application exceptions and native failures, including stack traces, installation/session identifiers, app and device details, game/renderer configuration, and available events preceding a failure. A rider crashing during normal gameplay is not itself an application crash report. Turning Analytics off does not turn Crashlytics off. We use diagnostics to investigate failures and maintain a reliable service.

7. How long information is kept

Category Retention period or criterion
Local saves Until removed through game/Android storage controls or uninstalling, subject to Android backup behaviour.
Cloud account and progress While the account is maintained, until an account-deletion request is fulfilled. There is no automatic inactivity-deletion schedule.
Wallet operation receipts Account-linked nested receipts are deleted during account deletion.
Purchase verification After deletion, account UID and order ID are removed from our purchase ledger. Minimal token hashes, product/amount, deletion/revocation status and timestamps remain for the verification service’s lifetime to prevent consumed or refunded purchases being reused. Hashed account-deletion markers remain to block stale requests. These records are pseudonymous, not a promise of anonymity.
Firebase Analytics User/event-level information expires under the rolling retention window configured for the Analytics property, including any configured reset on new activity. Aggregated reports can have different retention. Ask our privacy contact for the current property setting or a data-rights request; the window is not the same as the operational-log periods below.
Crashlytics Firebase states crash traces, extracted minidump information and associated identifiers are kept for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins. Native minidumps are held temporarily for processing.
Backend logs The configured default Google Cloud log bucket retains logs for 30 days. Required audit logs retain for 400 days. These periods do not describe every independent Google or GitHub service log.
Support and rights requests While handling the enquiry, then only for follow-up, resolving an open dispute, demonstrating fulfilment of a rights request, or an applicable legal obligation. Information that is no longer needed for those purposes should be removed during mailbox review; a legal hold can extend retention.

Provider deletion processing and backups may take additional time. Firebase states other Authentication information is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after the customer initiates user deletion. Google independently retains its own transaction records under its policies; deleting a game account does not delete them.

8. Account deletion and your choices

Request deletion in Settings → Google account → Delete account, or use our public account-deletion page without reinstalling the game. You may also email xavacki@gmail.com from the address used to sign in. We may verify ownership; do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Deletion removes the Firebase game account, cloud wallet, upgrades, race progress, level entitlement and nested account-linked operation receipts. It removes the account/order links from our purchase ledger, with the limited fraud-prevention retention described above. Paid coins and level access cannot be restored to a new game account. Account deletion does not delete your Google account or automatically refund purchases.

The requesting device clears account caches and queued operations after confirmation. Other devices clear their account caches when they reconnect and the old session is rejected; offline devices can retain cached information until reconnection or app-storage removal. Guest saves remain separate. Signing out and Reset all progress are not account deletion. Your optional Analytics choice remains a separate device setting; use Analytics & privacy to withdraw it.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Write to xavacki@gmail.com. We may request reasonable ownership verification. You may complain to your local supervisory authority. In Slovakia, contact the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic. Technical fraud/purchase checks do not remove your right to dispute an adjustment with us.

10. Audience, security and updates

Gravity Throttle is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. This does not override a country’s rules on minors’ consent or purchases. If you cannot independently give valid Analytics consent, choose No thanks unless an appropriate parent/guardian arrangement is in place. We do not provide an age-verification or parental-consent service merely through this notice. Contact us if you believe a child’s data has been collected improperly.

Cloud requests use HTTPS, signed-in operations require authentication, purchases are verified on the backend, and direct client database writes are restricted. No system is completely secure. We update this notice when practices change, state the effective date, and provide further notice or obtain consent where required.
